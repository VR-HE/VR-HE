    How to use this demo

    1. Give the view your keyboard

    Click once on the 3D view. While a panel or text box is open, the view ignores the keyboard and mouse, so typing never moves you.

    2. Move

    W / ↑ forward, S / ↓ back, A / ← step left, D / → step right. To turn, drag on the view (3). Hold Shift to go faster. Racks and walls stop you.

    3. Look around

    Hold the left mouse button on the view and drag. For game-style looking press M (or Capture mouse): the pointer disappears and a small cross marks the centre. Press Esc to get the pointer back.

    4. Camera height and reset

    Q raises the camera, E lowers it. X (or Reset view) restores the normal height and a level view; in third person it also puts the camera back behind the figure.

    5. First or third person

    V switches views. Third person shows a plain placeholder figure about 1.73 m tall as a rough size cue; drag to circle around it and use the mouse wheel to zoom.

    6. Select and open a record

    Point at a rack: a white outline appears. Click it: the outline turns cyan and its name shows above it. Then right-click it, press Enter, or press Open selected record. No mouse aiming needed: open Equipment list (L).

    7. Edit, save and reopen

    Type in Description and press Save (or Ctrl+Enter). Close the record and open it again, reload the page, or close the browser and start the demo again with the same launcher: the saved text returns. Each rack keeps its own text.

    8. What is saved, and where

    LOCAL DEMO DATA only: synthetic records in this browser profile on this computer. Nothing leaves this computer, and nothing goes to a spreadsheet or company system. Saved data & export downloads a JSON copy or resets the demo text. The demo's own test server on this computer keeps a copy of each export, with timings and console messages, in its results folder.

    Settings

    1.00x

    When on, moving the mouse up looks down, in both first and third person. Your choice is saved in this browser profile. Q/E camera height and X reset stay the same.

    Prototype notes: the room is synthetic; physical scale accuracy is not demonstrated. The frame rate shown is what the browser delivers at your display's refresh rate; no frame cap is set.
